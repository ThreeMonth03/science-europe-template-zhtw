    Section 3: Storage and backup during research process

    5. How will data and metadata be stored and backed up during the research process?

    There is a shared workspace used during the project for working with data.

    Dedicated specialists will operate the shared workspace.

    Storage needs will be the same during the whole project.

    All essential data will also be stored elsewhere to prevent a total loss of data.

    We will make automated backups of all data stored outside the workspace.

    Archival during the project

    Data will be archived in cold storage during the project.

    The archive will be stored on tape.

    The archive will be stored at a remote location.

    Changes to archived data will require re-archival.

    Frequent backups are required because the archived data changes frequently.

    These backups will be used to recover from accidental changes or deletions.
